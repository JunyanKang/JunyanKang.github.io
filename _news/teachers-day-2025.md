This illustrated team portrait captures a shared memory from **Teachers' Day 2025**.

Teachers' Day is an opportunity to thank the teachers and mentors who guide us, encourage our curiosity and help us grow as researchers. It is also a moment to appreciate the support and ideas we share with one another in the lab.

With gratitude to our mentors and every member of Kang Lab, we look forward to continuing to learn, ask questions and make discoveries together.
