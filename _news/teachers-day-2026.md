Ahead of **Teachers' Day 2026**, we gathered for a team photograph on **7 September**, with bouquets and warm wishes for our teachers and mentors.

Learning in the lab happens through experiments and discussions, but also through the patience, encouragement and experience shared by the people around us. This occasion gave us a chance to pause and say thank you.

To our mentors and colleagues: thank you for helping us grow, supporting our questions and making research a shared endeavour. Happy Teachers' Day!
