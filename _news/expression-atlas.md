The Kang Lab expression atlas provides a public entry point for exploring gene expression across **seven bulk, single-cell and spatial datasets**. No sign-in is required.

## Start with a gene

Search for a gene, choose a dataset, and examine the available expression views. Cross-species ortholog mapping helps users navigate corresponding genes in different species. Figures and data can be downloaded for further exploration.

## Interpret in context

The atlas is an exploration resource. Dataset-specific sampling, cell annotations and experimental methods should be considered when interpreting a pattern; expression alone does not establish a gene's causal function.

This spotlight introduces an existing lab resource rather than announcing a new experiment or a new dataset release. Related tools and workflows are available on the [software and data page](/resources/).
