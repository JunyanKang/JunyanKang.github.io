Our 2026 paper **DDX19B modulates post-meiotic 3′-UTR profile shifts in mice** is published in *Communications Biology*.

The study links the nuclear-pore-associated RNA helicase DDX19B to changes in 3′-UTR profiles during the transition from spermatocytes to spermatids. Loss of DDX19B alters these profiles and perturbs RNA-regulatory networks, supporting an indirect model of regulation. Cross-species analyses identify a shared shift toward proximal 3′-UTR usage in mouse, macaque and human.

**Authors:** Xinghai Yu, Zi-Wu Wang, Xinyu Liu, Ling Tong, Qing Li and Jun-Yan Kang. Xinghai Yu, Zi-Wu Wang and Xinyu Liu are joint first authors; Qing Li and Jun-Yan Kang are corresponding authors.
