Kang Lab took part in the **30th Congress of Chinese Ophthalmological Society (CCOS2026)**, held on **9–12 September 2026** at the **National Convention & Exhibition Center (Tianjin)**. The meeting was organised by the Chinese Medical Association and its Chinese Ophthalmological Society.

## Our contributions

| Lab member | Contribution |
| --- | --- |
| Lu-Yue Ding | Poster presentation |
| Yuan-Rong Guo | Poster presentation |

The meeting provided an opportunity to share our research with the wider ophthalmology community and learn from work across vision science and clinical ophthalmology.
