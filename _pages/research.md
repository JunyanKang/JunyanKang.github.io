<section class="kl-research-section" id="retina">
<span class="kl-number">01 / PRIMARY RESEARCH FOCUS</span>
<h2>Retinal development, aging & evolution</h2>
<p>We are interested in how retinal cell types are generated, how their molecular states change with age, and which features are conserved or divergent across species. These questions guide our use of molecular biology, single-cell and spatial expression analysis, and functional studies.</p>
<div class="kl-question-grid"><article><span class="kl-number">DEVELOPMENT</span><h3>Building cell diversity</h3><p>How do retinal progenitors generate different cell identities, and how are differentiation programs coordinated?</p></article><article><span class="kl-number">AGING</span><h3>Changing through time</h3><p>How do retinal cell states and regulatory programs change with age, and what may underlie selective vulnerability?</p></article><article><span class="kl-number">EVOLUTION</span><h3>Comparing across species</h3><p>Which molecular programs are shared across species, and how do retinal cell types and visual specializations diverge?</p></article></div>

<span class="kl-number">02 / RNA BIOLOGY</span><h2>Post-transcriptional control of differentiation</h2>
<p>Our publication record spans piRNA-mediated mRNA regulation, translation of stored mRNAs, biomolecular condensation and 3′-UTR regulation. These studies provide a foundation for investigating how gene expression is coordinated with cellular differentiation.</p>

<span class="kl-number">03 / CELL FATE</span><h2>Molecular transitions and chromatin regulation</h2>
