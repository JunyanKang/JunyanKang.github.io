# Kang Lab Website

Public laboratory website at https://kanglab.cool, built with Jekyll and an
independent React expression atlas, published through the existing GitHub Pages
workflow. Content is maintained in Pages CMS. No hosting migration is intended.

Visitors are researchers, collaborators, students and applicants. Their primary
tasks are finding research and publications, querying expression data, discovering
software and external resources, meeting the team and contacting the laboratory.

Home introduces the research with a real CellScope-derived 3D UMAP animation,
then current news. Its dark steel visual system continues through every page,
with layered panels for reading, resources and the expression workbench.
The media alternates cell-type colors and measured marker-gene expression; it is
an embedding visualization, not an anatomical reconstruction. Source matrices
remain private and unchanged. Research, publications and news
prioritize reading; resources prioritize discovery; the atlas prioritizes task
completion. Keep them visually related without forcing a marketing layout into
the data tool. Use existing content rather than inventing claims or news.

Acceptance requires working desktop and mobile navigation, no page-level
horizontal overflow, accessible search/filter states, retained CMS edit tests,
unaltered raw dataset checksums, a full build and a verified Pages deployment.
