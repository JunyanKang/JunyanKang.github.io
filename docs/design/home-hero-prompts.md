# Homepage Hero Art Direction

These are conceptual editorial illustrations, not microscopy, quantitative plots,
or evidence for the papers' conclusions. Generated with the built-in image tool.
Titles and navigation are accessible HTML, never baked into the images.

## Delivery

- Built-in image generation was used, with no external API-key workflow.
- Website assets: `assets/img/hero/retinal-identity.webp`,
  `assets/img/hero/rna-regulation.webp`, `assets/img/hero/spatial-atlas.webp`.
- Full-resolution PNG copies: `output/home-refresh/originals/` (not published).
- Final retinal-art correction prompt: Preserve composition, cell positions,
  teal/sage/amber/ivory colors and editorial style. Remove the DNA-like helix
  from the amber photoreceptor outer/inner segment; replace it with subtle
  parallel stacked membrane-disc-like lines, tapered for the cone. Do not place
  DNA or nuclei in outer segments. Chromatin hints may remain only in the
  rounded nucleated cell-body layer. No text, labels, arrows or added elements.
- Covers are conceptual editorial art, explicitly identified as such on the
  news pages; they are not presented as experimental figures or activity photos.

## 1

Use case: scientific-educational. Asset type: premium academic retinal-biology laboratory website hero illustration, landscape 4:3, at least 1536 pixels wide. Primary request: an original sophisticated editorial image about retinal cell identity, development, and molecular diversity, for Kang Lab. Scene: warm ivory paper-like atmospheric background, not literal paper texture. Subject: a gently curving ribbon of retinal tissue viewed obliquely, with a restrained coherent arrangement of tall rods and tapered cones in the upper portion, compact nucleated cells beneath and a subtle branching neural layer at the lower edge. A single warm amber cone among muted teal rods provides a focal point; fine molecular dots and a faint organized chromatin-like thread inside a few enlarged translucent cell bodies hint at gene regulation, not a quantitative experiment. Medium: beautifully crafted flat-to-soft-dimensional scientific editorial illustration, rounded clean silhouettes, delicate linework, matte glass accents, no glossy plastic. Composition: dynamic curved tissue flows from lower left toward upper right, focal details in upper and central two thirds; leave lower fifth quiet for website HTML title overlay; wide balanced margins, no important subject clipped. Palette: deep teal #005865, sage green #91B5A0, warm amber #D99024, creamy ivory #F5F2EA; very limited contrast accents. Constraints: conceptual rather than anatomical plate, coherent layering, no literal eyeball, no brain icon, no arrows, no charts, no axes, no heatmaps, no fake data, no people, no text, no letters, no labels, no logos, no watermark. Make it visually substantial and elegant at a 570px-wide website display rather than a busy textbook diagram.

## 2

Use case: scientific-educational. Asset type: premium academic molecular-cell-biology website hero illustration, landscape 4:3, at least 1536 pixels wide. Primary request: visually communicate post-transcriptional RNA regulation and the fate of messenger RNA without presenting a specific unverified molecular mechanism. Scene: warm ivory luminous cytoplasmic space with subtle depth and generous breathing room. Subject: one flowing amber single RNA ribbon with modest bead-like nucleotide rhythm, passing near a small family of rounded teal RNA-binding protein forms and one soft translucent biomolecular condensate; a second quieter strand curves away to imply different RNA fates. A restrained two-lobed abstract ribosome form may sit on one RNA segment, but no invented helicase structure, no atomistically exact protein claims, no DNA double helix masquerading as RNA. Style: scientifically literate editorial illustration, matte translucent forms, crisp restrained contours, gentle shadows, delicate sage fine line accents, no glossy neon or sci-fi visuals. Composition: looping diagonal flow through center and upper half, organized clusters rather than confetti, a large quiet lower fifth reserved for HTML overlay; crop-safe important subjects. Palette: deep teal #005865, muted sage #91B5A0, amber #D99024, ivory #F5F2EA. Constraints: conceptual visual only, not a data panel or mechanistic proof; no labels, words, symbols, numbers, arrows, charts, axes, watermark, branding or borders. Match a refined retinal research lab identity, not a pharmaceutical advertisement.

## 3

Use case: scientific-educational. Asset type: premium academic expression-atlas website hero illustration, landscape 4:3, at least 1536 pixels wide. Primary request: convey linking cell identity, tissue context and gene expression through a shared research atlas. Scene: airy warm ivory background with a subtle architectural arrangement of three translucent gently curved tissue slices, aligned in depth. Subject: each slice contains an organized mosaic of softly outlined rounded cells with small nuclei; a small number of amber cells are highlighted consistently among sage and teal cells. Fine delicate connector threads link corresponding conceptual cell identities between adjacent slices, without arrowheads. Include a graceful retinal tissue arc within one slice so the connection to retinal biology is clear. Treat the slices as abstract spatial context, not as actual microscopy or measured expression. Style: refined editorial scientific illustration, planar layers plus soft depth, matte translucent materials and clean natural contours. Composition: central upper-two-thirds sculptural arrangement, balanced asymmetry, open quiet lower fifth for HTML text overlay, safe margins. Palette: deep teal #005865, sage #91B5A0, warm amber #D99024, warm ivory #F5F2EA. No axes, grids of numbers, plots, UMAPs, heatmaps, scale bars, rendered UI, labels, text, letters, people, logos or watermarks. It should look like a visual invitation to explore a research resource, not fabricated experimental evidence.
